# Vortex-Thread Motion 1.0.0

This research edition turns the supplied discussion draft into a reproducible analytic-field and compression-baseline package. It preserves the pressure and segment-width formulas, makes nondimensionalization and energy normalization explicit, and separates completed verification from the physical and compression hypotheses that remain untested.

Included: eight-page PDF and editable manuscript, ten research references, three source-driven figures in PNG and SVG, exact-field and RK4 code, reversible XOR packet baselines, pinned-fixture fetcher, and a separate standard-library packet checker.

Local validation passed 20 unittest methods and eight separately counted numerical acceptance checks. All 18 freshly measured packets reconstructed their canonical targets byte-for-byte, and an independent decoder reproduced those checks. The wrong-pressure and skipped-suite negative controls correctly failed. Source and archive identity are recorded by the release manifest.

The original authored 3D stream, decoder and media were absent from the supplied publishing kit. Their earlier replay and compression results are not presented as reproduced in this edition. No numerical 3D fluid solver, held-out flow predictor, general compression advantage or independent human peer review is claimed.

Public release and final reuse terms remain pending the author's explicit license selection. Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.24574783` is reserved, not yet registered as a published record.
