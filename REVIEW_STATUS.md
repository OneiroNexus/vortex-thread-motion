# Review status - version 1.0.0

Aaron Michael Weis | Research edition 1.0.0 | 2026-11-26

The bounded computational checks below passed. The framework connects particle collection and motion compression through organized motion, with separate unvalidated test plans. Physical collection requires retained-mass, mass-balance and energy measurements; data compression requires a flow predictor plus reversible residuals to beat prespecified baselines on held-out data. Neither goal is established by the current supporting checks. Particle collection must not be equated with reducing fluid volume.
